Everything good starts a little off-centre.

StayTilted began with a habit: leaving the horizon crooked on purpose. The people we photograph aren't perfectly level either, so the pictures shouldn't pretend.

We work quietly, direct as little as we can, and edit with a light hand. True colour, honest skin, real light.

Before you book

Tell us the plan. A date, a place and a rough idea is plenty.

We agree the details. Coverage, locations and delivery, written down plainly.

You get the gallery. Edited pictures in a private online gallery, ready to download and share.

How far ahead should I book?

As early as you can. Weekends and popular seasons fill first, but ask even if your date is close.

Do you travel?

Yes. Tell us where, and we'll sort the details out in your quote.

Can I get the unedited files?

We deliver edited pictures only, so everything you receive looks the way we intend it to.

What if I hate being photographed?

Most people do. We keep the camera moving and the instructions light, so you mostly forget it's there.

Tell us about your shoot.

Share the date, the place and what you have in mind. We'll write back with ideas and a quote.
