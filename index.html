Ask us directly ↗
I feel awkward in front of a camera. Now what?

Same as most people, honestly. We keep things moving, give you something to do, and never expect you to magically know how to pose. You can take a breather whenever you need one.

Can we shoot somewhere other than a studio?

Absolutely. Your favourite coffee shop, a quiet bit of coastline, a convention floor, your own living room—we’ll find a place that feels like you. On-location sessions travel free within 15km of Springs, then R5 per km after that.

Can I bring costume changes, props, or a friend?

Please do. Bring the outfit, the prop, the hype person, and anything else that helps you feel at home in front of the camera. Additional studio props may be made available on request.

How much does a shoot cost?

Packages start at R700. Have a look at the packages above, or tell us a little about the idea and we’ll confirm everything before anything is booked.

How do bookings and deposits work?

Bookings are by appointment only. A deposit confirms your booking and is non-refundable if cancelled with less than 5 days’ notice, but may be transferred to a new date once, subject to availability.

Is there anything to sign?

Everyone on set signs an indemnity waiver beforehand. Damage to the set, studio or props will incur a fee.

When will my photos be ready?

We’ll give you a delivery window before we shoot and keep you in the loop. No vanishing acts, no mystery waiting game.

Can I ask for changes to my edited photos?

Edited photos include one (1) free revision. Multiple revisions will be charged for.

Can I use the photos commercially?

All photos paid for are free for the models and photographers to use for commercial purposes without mandatory credit, but credit is appreciated.
